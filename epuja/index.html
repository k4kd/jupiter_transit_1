🌑 SOMVATI AMAVASYA 2026 🌑

Somvati
Amavasya

Amavasya is the New Moon day. It is the darkest night of the month. The moon is not seen in the sky.

Somvar means Monday — the day of Lord Shiva. When Amavasya falls on a Monday, it is called Somvati Amavasya. This is very rare. In a whole year, it comes only once or twice.

This day is believed to carry the blessings of both Lord Shiva and our ancestors (Pitra). Prayers offered with faith on this day are said to travel far.

The Significance

Why Is This Day So Special?

🌑
New Moon = Thin Veil. Amavasya is when the moon disappears. It is believed that on this day, the gap between our world and the world of our ancestors becomes very thin.
🙏
Monday = Lord Shiva's Day. Every Monday is special for Lord Shiva. When Amavasya falls on a Monday, the blessings of both come together. Prayers to Shiva on this day are said to be very powerful.
👴
Best Day for Pitra Puja. Our ancestors (Pitra) are believed to be closer to us on Amavasya. Offering prayers for them on this day brings peace to their souls — and good things to our families.
📖
Written in the Mahabharata. Bhishma Pitamah himself told Yudhishthira: "Observe Somvati Amavasya Vrat. It will bring noble children to your family and make your dynasty strong again."

How to Observe

The Traditional Way to Observe This Day

1
Find a Peepal tree. Walk around it 108 times. As you walk, tie a sacred thread (Mauli) around the trunk. This is called Parikrama.
2
Offer milk, flowers, sandal paste, and sindoor (red powder) to the Peepal tree. These are sacred offerings believed to please the divine energy of the tree.
3
Keep a fast for the whole day. Do not eat food. Fasting is said to purify the body and mind — and bring the person closer to God.
4
Give white things to the poor — rice, curd, sugar, or milk. Donating on this day is believed to bring good fortune back to you.
The Sacred Story

The Stories Behind This Day

📖 From the Mahabharata
After the great Kurukshetra war ended, Yudhishthira was very sad. He was worried about the future of his family and the Kuru dynasty. He went to Bhishma Pitamah, who lay on a bed of arrows, to ask for help.

Bhishma Pitamah told him: "Observe the Somvati Amavasya Vrat with full faith. This fast will bring noble children to your family. Your dynasty will grow strong and prosper again."

This is why Somvati Amavasya has been observed with deep faith for thousands of years across India.
🕊️ The Story of the Girl
A monk used to visit a rich family every day. He always blessed their daughters-in-law — but never the family's daughter. The girl and her mother felt hurt and worried.

A Pandit read the girl's Kundali (birth chart) and found a serious Dosha — a shadow over her future marriage. He said: "Your daughter must travel to the island of Singhal. She must find a washerwoman there, get sindoor from her, and observe the Somvati Amavasya fast."

The girl went with her younger brother. At the seashore, they rested under a tree. A snake was attacking baby vultures in their nest. The girl and her brother bravely saved the chicks. In return, the grateful vultures helped them cross the sea.

The girl served the washerwoman with great care for many months. Happy with her devotion, the woman applied sindoor on the girl's forehead. On her way back, the girl observed the Somvati Amavasya fast — without even drinking water.

The Dosha was fully removed. She went on to live a long and happy married life.
